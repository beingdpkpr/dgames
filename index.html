Batting only. Score as many as you can before the overs run out or you lose 5 wickets.

Aim — hold arrow keys or WASD (combine two for diagonals). The arrow on the turf and the radar show the shot direction.
Ground shot — Space.   Lofted shot — Enter (or Shift).
Timing — the bar under the pitch fills as the ball comes in; press as the marker reaches the green. Early drags to leg, late goes to off. Outside the yellow you may edge it or lose your stumps.
Short balls suit cuts and pulls. Full balls suit drives. Bouncers over your head cannot be hit.
Esc — pause, then resume or quit to this menu.
