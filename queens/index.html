How to play

  • Place one queen in every row, every column and every colour region.
  • Queens may not touch each other, not even at a corner.
  • Tap once for an × (“no queen here”), twice for a queen, three times to clear. Drag to paint ×s.
  • Every puzzle has exactly one answer, and you can always reach it by logic, without guessing. Stuck? A hint shows the next deduction and why.

Keyboard: arrows move, Space cycles, X marks, Q places, H hints, Ctrl+Z undoes.

Settings
